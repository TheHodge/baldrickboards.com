---
title: Baldrick8 Introduction
---

Used in installations around the world, the Baldrick8 is our flagship controller, powering everything from light-up shelves to theatre productions. 

With our custom hardware and firmware, this small board packs a punch when it comes to features and can go toe to toe with boards of all sizes and prices.

* Dual Power Inputs
  * Each input powers 4 pixel ports
  * Supports 5V to 48V inputs, or 5V to 24V on 1.0 versions
  * Reverse polarity protection
  * Each port is fused with 7.5A fuses
  * Independent voltage management, so you can run 5V on one side and 24V on the other
  * Advanced power management
  * **With higher voltage support, dual inputs and reverse polarity protection, our power inputs allow longer runs whilst keeping your setup protected**

* 8 Pixel Outputs
  * Supports 2,250 channels per port, which is 750 RGB pixels, at 40 FPS
  * RGBW pixels supported
  * Works with WS2811-compatible pixels
  * Also supports UCS1903, SM16703P and UCS2904 protocols
  * *Additional protocols are always being added to our firmware*
  * **With the Baldrick8, you get full access to 750 pixels per port at 40 FPS. No banks, no pixel sharing, no rotary dials, just plug and play**

* Flexible Input Support
  * Compatible with DDP, Art-Net, E1.31 and sACN
  * Designed to integrate with industry-standard lighting desks, including ChamSys and MA Lighting
  * Built into xLights as a controller, ILightThat/Baldrick8, making setup and pushing configuration simple
  * Customers have showcased the Baldrick8 using MadMapper, ELM (Enttec), LOR, sacnView, Resolume Arena and more (just don't ask us for technical support with them!) 
  * **Even without direct integration, we’ve designed the Baldrick8 to work with common protocols used in home shows, the events industry and everything in between**

* 3 Input Ports
  * Designed to work with almost any input: buttons, motion sensors, reed switches, step plates and more. As long as it connects a circuit, it can work
  * Connects to our Turnip Network to easily add interactivity to your event
  * Automatically finds and configures other Baldrick Boards, allowing you to control effects and ports from them
  * Codeless FPP integration lets you start and stop the show, change the volume and play a random song without any technical knowledge
  * Easy-to-use built-in defaults for configuration, testing and those “oh no” moments
  * **You might not have a use for the inputs yet, but when adding interactivity becomes as easy as adding a button and clicking a few links, they can quickly come in handy**

* The Baldrick8 is a *Medium* size board.
	* Baldrick boards currently come in Small, Medium and Large. 
	* All of our boards have 3D printable mounts available for free on the Baldrick Website
	* **Baldrick Boards follow a size guide allowing you to easily create and reuse mounts, containers and fixings**

* The CunningFX onboard engine
  * Allows for static lighting effects perfect for houses, interactive stations or art installations
  * With multiple presets, layering and over 10 effects all easy to use.
  * **CunningFX allows you to use your controller without a show player and get something pretty impressive**
