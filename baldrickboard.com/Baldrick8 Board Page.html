FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How many pixels can the Baldrick8 drive?
Each of the 8 ports handles up to 2250 channels — that's 750 RGB pixels at 40fps per port, for a high-refresh, flicker-free show.
Do I need a Raspberry Pi or an FPP licence?
No. The Baldrick8 runs standalone with its built-in web interface — no Pi and no FPP licence required.
What power input does it take?
5–48V DC (up to 48V on v1.1) across two inputs, with onboard power management and the option for individual voltages per section.
Which software does it work with?
It's DDP, Art-Net, E1.31 and sACN compatible, with push-from-xLights integration for a fast workflow.
